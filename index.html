Population genomics · Museum für Naturkunde, Berlin

Dr. Joshua Peñalba

evolution · genomics · speciation

I study how genome divergence unfolds during speciation — comparing replicate population pairs across the divergence continuum, mostly in Carabus ground beetles, to build a fuller picture of how species form.

Research

3 areas

Hybridization and speciation

How selection, drift, and gene flow — shaped by demography, geography, and incompatibilities — govern divergence across the speciation continuum, using replicate hybrid and suture zones as natural experiments.

Comparative population genomics

Building and using high-quality reference genomes — combining short- and long-read data — to compare population-level divergence across taxa and ask what's general about speciation.

Museomics

Extracting genomic data from museum specimens to extend sampling across time and space, broadening the taxa and populations that can be studied.
